Yes. If I were coaching someone with this resume, I'd approach it with one goal:

By the end of prep, there should be no line on the resume that an experienced systems engineer can use to corner you.

This isn't about memorizing RFCs. It's about being able to explain why things work, why you built them that way, and what trade-offs you made.

Level 0: Know your own projects cold

For every bullet on the resume, ask yourself:

Why did I build this?
Why this architecture?
What alternatives did I reject?
What was the hardest bug?
What bottleneck did I hit?
What would I redesign today?
What assumptions did I make?
What limitations does it have?

If someone points to literally any sentence on your resume, you should comfortably talk for 5–10 minutes about it.

A time you disagreed with advice or documentation and why.
What you'd do differently if you rebuilt each project today.

SECTION 30 — "Why?"

This is the section that separates people who used a technology from people who understood it. For every technology on your resume, be ready for questions like:

Why did you choose this over the alternatives?
What trade-offs did you accept?
What limitations did you encounter?
What would happen if you replaced it with X?
What are the performance implications?
What are the failure modes?
How would you scale it 100×?
What security considerations did you account for?
If I were actually interviewing you...

I'd estimate this question bank is 250–400 individual questions, and that's about right for a resume with this level of depth. If you can work through all of these and answer confidently—not from memorization, but from understanding—you'll be in a strong position for interviews focused on systems, networking, infrastructure, and distributed backend engineering.
